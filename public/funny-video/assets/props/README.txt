Drop your prop cut-outs here. Everything is optional - anything missing is
drawn by the app's built-in canvas painters (js/props.js).

File names (extension can be .png, .webp, .jpg or .jpeg):
  grass, flowers, lamp, heart, star, balloons, tree, bike, car,
  moon, cloud, wedding, confetti, sparkle

  e.g. flowers.png   or   flowers.webp

Variants (optional): add flowers-2.png / flowers-3.png next to flowers.png and
all of them get mixed across the frame. Numbering starts at -2, max 3 per prop.

Tips:
  - transparent background (PNG/WebP with alpha)
  - ~300-500 px wide is plenty; "wedding" is a full-width banner, ~1080 px
  - draw upright and centred: ground props (grass, flowers, tree, bike, car)
    are anchored by their bottom edge, hanging props (lamp, wedding) by the top

Press "Check my assets" in the app to see exactly which files were picked up.
